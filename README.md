## 📝 Publications & Research

### �� LiDARDraft: Generating LiDAR Point Cloud from Versatile Inputs
**Under Review @ AAAI 2026** · *First Author* · 2024.06 – 2025.06

Unified text, image, and point cloud into a shared **3D layout** representation, converted into semantic and depth control signals. Adopted **ControlNet** to guide unconditional LiDAR generation based on **Stable Diffusion**, achieving high-quality "zero-to-simulation" generation with both flexibility and geometric consistency.

**Keywords:** `Stable Diffusion` `ControlNet` `LiDAR Generation` `3D Layout` `Multi-modal Input`

---

### 🧭 GeoNLF: Geometry-guided Pose-Free Neural LiDAR Fields
**Accepted @ NeurIPS 2025** · *Fourth Author* · 2023.09 – 2024.05

Proposed a hybrid framework alternating between neural reconstruction and geometric pose optimization. Introduced **selective weighted geometry constraints** to improve pose-free NeRF optimization stability.

**Keywords:** `NeRF` `Pose-Free` `Neural Reconstruction` `Geometry Optimization`

---

### 🌈 MUP: Multimodal LiDAR–Camera Novel View Synthesis
**Accepted @ NeurIPS 2026** · *Fifth Author* · 2024.05 – 2025.05

Developed **MUP (Multimodal Unified Pose-free)**, a LiDAR–camera fusion framework for large-scale view synthesis without pose supervision. Provided continuous depth supervision through LiDAR fields, improving robustness of pose-free image synthesis.

**Keywords:** `LiDAR-Camera Fusion` `Novel View Synthesis` `Pose-Free` `Depth Supervision`

---

### 🎥 Efficient Trajectory-Conditioned Text-to-4D Gaussian Splatting
**Submitted @ ICRA 2026** · *Third Author* · 2024.09 – 2025.08

Explored **4D trajectory-conditioned video generation** using HexPlane spatiotemporal representations combined with Stable Diffusion, DreamGaussian, and Stable Video Diffusion. Achieved geometrically and temporally consistent video synthesis aligned with trajectory inputs.

**Keywords:** `4D Generation` `Gaussian Splatting` `HexPlane` `Trajectory-Conditioned`
